Practice Area

Family Law

Sensitive, experienced handling of matrimonial and family disputes.

Family law matters — divorce, custody, maintenance, domestic violence — are among the most personally difficult legal proceedings anyone can face. They require not only legal expertise but also sensitivity, discretion, and a commitment to achieving outcomes that protect long-term well-being. At Advocates & Associates, we bring two decades of experience in matrimonial litigation before the Bombay Family Court, Sessions Court, and the Bombay High Court, handled with the seriousness and care each matter deserves.

Our divorce practice covers both contested divorce petitions and mutual consent divorce under the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955, along with matters under Muslim Personal Law, Special Marriage Act, and Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act. We advise clients thoroughly on the grounds available, the realistic timelines involved, and the strategic considerations before initiating proceedings. Where mutual consent is possible, we guide clients through the process to achieve a swift and dignified resolution.

Child custody and guardianship matters require an especially careful approach, as the welfare of the child remains the paramount consideration under Indian law. We represent clients seeking custody, visitation rights, and modification of existing custody orders — appearing before the Family Court, the District Court, and the Guardian and Wards Court as required. We ensure that our clients' rights are fully argued without unnecessarily exposing children to prolonged adversarial proceedings.

Maintenance applications under Section 125 of the Code of Criminal Procedure and Section 24 of the Hindu Marriage Act are handled with urgency, as financial support is often the most pressing immediate need. We also handle cases under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005, including applications for protection orders, residence orders, and compensation orders, ensuring that our clients receive the full benefit of available legal protections without delay.
