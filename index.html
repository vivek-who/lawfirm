Common Questions

Everything You Need
to Know

Our chambers handles a broad range of legal matters. On the criminal side, we appear in bail proceedings, trial matters before the Sessions Court, and criminal appeals at the Bombay High Court. For civil disputes — contracts, recovery, injunctions, title — we handle the full lifecycle of litigation. Family cases including divorce, maintenance under Section 125 CrPC, and custody disputes are a significant part of our practice. We also handle property and RERA matters, cheque bounce cases under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, cyber crime complaints under the IT Act, and general corporate advisory work. If you're unsure whether your matter falls within our scope, the first consultation will clarify that at no charge.

The first consultation is free. We believe you should be able to speak with a lawyer and understand your legal position before committing to any fees. After the initial meeting, if you wish to engage us, our fees are discussed transparently — we explain exactly what you're paying for, when, and why. There are no surprise bills mid-matter. For clients with limited means and genuine cases, we are open to discussing suitable arrangements.

Honestly, it depends on the nature and complexity of the matter. As a general guide: bail applications in criminal matters can be resolved within days to a few weeks. Cheque bounce trials under Section 138 NI Act typically take 6 to 18 months depending on the court's workload and whether the matter is contested. Civil suits — property disputes, recovery suits, injunctions — tend to run anywhere from 1 to 3 years in the trial court, though interim reliefs like stays can often be secured quickly. We will give you a realistic time estimate for your specific matter during the consultation, not a number designed to make you feel better.

Our primary court of appearance is the Bombay High Court, and we regularly appear before the Sessions Courts, Family Courts, and various Tribunals across Maharashtra. For matters in other states, we work through a trusted network of associate advocates in key jurisdictions — Delhi, Pune, Nagpur, and others — ensuring that you receive the same standard of representation without disruption. We will always be transparent about which matters we handle directly and which involve associates.

Absolutely. Consultations are available in both Hindi and English. Legal matters are stressful enough — you should not also be struggling to explain your situation in a language that isn't your first. Adv. Vivek is completely comfortable conducting the full consultation, case discussion, and follow-up communication in Hindi. Many of our clients from across Maharashtra and neighbouring states prefer this, and we accommodate that without hesitation.

Bring whatever documents are directly related to your matter — the more complete your file, the more useful the consultation will be. This typically includes: any legal notices sent or received, prior court orders or FIR copies, agreements or contracts in dispute, property documents if relevant, and your government-issued ID. It also helps greatly if you write down a brief timeline of events before you come in — when things happened, who was involved, and what the key turning points were. If you don't have all documents yet, come anyway. We can guide you on what to gather and how to proceed.
