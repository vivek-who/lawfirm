Practice Area

Cyber Crime

Legal protection against digital fraud, online harassment and IT Act offences.

As digital transactions and online communication have become central to daily life, so too has the risk of cyber crime. From UPI fraud and phishing attacks to social media defamation and corporate data breaches, cyber offences cut across every segment of society and can cause significant financial and reputational harm. Advocates & Associates provides legal assistance across the full range of cyber crime matters under the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the allied provisions of the Indian Penal Code.

Online financial fraud — including UPI scams, OTP fraud, investment fraud through fake trading platforms, and phishing — is among the most common categories of cyber crime. We assist victims in filing complaints with the Cyber Cell of the Mumbai Police, the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal, and the appropriate court, while also advising on parallel civil remedies to recover lost funds. Prompt action is critical in these matters, and we are equipped to assist clients urgently from the moment a fraud is discovered.

Cyber stalking, harassment, and morphing of images are offences under both the IT Act and the IPC, and victims are entitled to strong legal protection including restraining orders, criminal prosecution, and compensation. We assist victims in documenting digital evidence — essential in all cyber crime matters — and in navigating the investigation process with the police, including through formal representations to senior officers when complaints are not being acted upon.

Corporate and business entities face increasing threats from data theft, unauthorized access to systems, and insider breaches. Under Section 66 and related provisions of the IT Act, as well as under the IPC, these offences attract criminal liability. We advise businesses on their legal options upon discovery of a breach, including criminal complaints, civil suits for damages, and interim injunctions restraining the continued use or circulation of stolen data.
