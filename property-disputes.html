Practice Area

Property Disputes

Expert property litigation across Maharashtra including RERA matters.

Property disputes in Maharashtra are among the most complex and financially significant legal matters individuals and families face. From ancestral land disputes and partition suits to modern builder-buyer conflicts under RERA, property litigation demands meticulous document analysis and procedural rigour. Advocates & Associates has extensive experience in property-related litigation before the Civil Court, District Court, Bombay High Court, and the Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority (MahaRERA).

Title disputes — establishing, defending, or challenging ownership of property — form the core of our property practice. We handle suits for declaration of title, cancellation of forged or fraudulent sale deeds, and cases involving benami transactions under the Prohibition of Benami Property Transactions Act. Thorough chain-of-title examination and strategic use of documentary evidence are the foundation of every case we build.

Possession and eviction matters, including suits under the Specific Relief Act for recovery of immovable property from unauthorised occupants, encroachers, or licensees who refuse to vacate, are handled aggressively on behalf of property owners. We also appear in proceedings under the Maharashtra Rent Control Act in landlord-tenant disputes involving residential and commercial tenancies, including eviction on grounds of default, bona fide requirement, and illegal subletting.

Our RERA practice covers complaints against builders for delay in possession, failure to deliver promised amenities, deviation from approved building plans, and defective construction. We assist homebuyers throughout the MahaRERA complaint process, from filing to recovery of compensation and interest, ensuring that the statutory protections afforded under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, are fully enforced in our clients' favour.
