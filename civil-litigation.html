Practice Area

Civil Litigation

Comprehensive civil litigation across property, recovery, and declaratory matters.

Civil litigation encompasses the broadest range of legal disputes outside the criminal system — from recovery of money lent to complex multi-party property suits to injunctions restraining wrongful acts. Advocates & Associates handles civil matters across the entire lifecycle, from institution of the suit to final execution of the decree, with a focus on achieving results efficiently without unnecessary prolongation of proceedings.

Our civil practice includes suits for recovery of money and enforcement of contractual obligations, including summary suits under Order 37 of the Code of Civil Procedure, which provide an expedited track for commercial recovery matters where the defendant has no triable defence. We regularly appear before the City Civil Court, District Courts, and the Bombay High Court on its original side for high-value civil disputes involving parties across Maharashtra and beyond.

In the area of injunctions, we handle both temporary and permanent injunction applications in disputes involving breach of contract, intellectual property, real estate, and business relationships. We understand the urgency that often accompanies injunction applications, and we are equipped to move applications on short notice when immediate judicial intervention is required to prevent irreparable harm.

Execution of court decrees is a frequently neglected but critical final step in civil litigation. A decree in your favour is only as valuable as your ability to enforce it. We handle execution proceedings — attachment of property, garnishee orders, arrest of judgment debtors — with the same attention to strategy and procedure as the original trial. Our clients are represented at every stage until actual recovery is achieved.
